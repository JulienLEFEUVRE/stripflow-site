Privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit this website or contact us, why we collect it, and how to exercise your rights, including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for visitors located in the European Union.

Data controller

Neora Group LLC, 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States. Contact: contact@stripsflow.com.

Representative in the European Union (Article 27 GDPR): to be appointed.

Data we collect

  • Browsing the website: we collect no data. The website uses no cookies or trackers that require consent, no analytics and no forms, and its fonts are hosted on the website itself. Our hosting provider may process technical data (IP address, browser type) to keep the website secure and running.
  • Booking a call: if you book a call, you provide your name, email address and, if you wish, information about your needs, through Calendly.
  • Email: the information you send us (identity, company, description of your needs).

Purposes and legal basis

  • Answering your request and organizing the call: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Keeping the website secure: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Providing this data is necessary to answer your request. We never sell your data or use it for advertising. We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling.

Retention

Prospect data is kept for no more than 3 years after our last exchange. Client data is kept for the duration of the business relationship, then archived for the period required by law.

Recipients and service providers

  • GitHub, Inc. (United States): website hosting (GitHub Pages).
  • Calendly LLC (United States): call scheduling.
  • Our email provider, for email exchanges.

Neora Group LLC is based in the United States, so the data you send us is processed there. For its US service providers, Neora relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You can request a copy of these safeguards by emailing us.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to and port your data. To exercise these rights, email us at the address above. We answer within one month.

If you are in the European Union and believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr).

Client data

During an engagement, the company data processed by the tools we set up is covered by a separate data processing agreement, which specifies the services used, where data is processed and how long it is kept.
