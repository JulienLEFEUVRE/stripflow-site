FAQ

What people ask us before getting started.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Your team gives instructions in plain English, the way they would brief a colleague. We handle the installation and the technical setup.

Why Claude Code and Codex instead of ChatGPT?

A chatbot answers in a window, and you have to copy and paste everything. Claude Code and Codex are agents: they read your files, apply your brand guidelines and deliver finished work directly in your folders and tools.

Can AI really replace a marketing team?

It handles the production: content strategy, copywriting, repurposing, scripts, emails, reports. You, or one person on your team, keep the direction: you set the goals, review and approve before anything is published.

Do you work with e-commerce brands too?

Yes. Shopify stores or other platforms: we connect AI to your store, your ad campaigns and your email marketing tool, then teach you to produce content, ads and emails in-house.

What happens to my data?

We use the vendors' business plans and decide with you what gets shared. Every setting is documented to support your compliance with GDPR and US privacy requirements.

How much does it cost?

Two line items: the tool subscriptions, at a fixed monthly price, and our setup and support services. You get a detailed quote after the 30-minute audit, before any commitment.

Do you work with US companies?

Yes. We work with companies in the United States and France. Setup, training and support are done remotely over video calls, with secure access to your machines.
